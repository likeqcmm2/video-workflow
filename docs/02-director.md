# Phase 2 — Director notes (the GENRE decides the PICTURES)

Work like a director, not a template filler. Test 1: mute any 10 seconds — a viewer must guess the genre.
Test 2: listen to any 30 seconds — someone who knows the channel must recognise its voice (Phase 1).
Code-built graphics are a tool, not a default: when real footage of the thing being said exists, it wins.

Write into script.md (after the profile), before any narration:
- genre (main + borrowed elements), the main emotion (fear, curiosity, awe, excitement, grief…)
- planned share of real footage/photos vs graphics → `project.yaml director.target_real_pct`
- pace (slow-heavy / medium / fast), cuts and transitions, on-screen text style, colour, sound
- three things NOT to do in this film
- long films (>15 min): how visuals stay varied per chapter (chapter cards, a different dominant source per
  chapter, a recurring graphic that evolves)

| Genre | Dominant picture | Graphics for | Pace & motion | Look preset | Avoid |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| True crime, disaster, news | 70–90 % real footage/photos: scene, broadcasts, court, people, documents, newspapers | only when no footage exists: maps, law text, sentences, simple timelines | slow, heavy; hard cuts, fade through black between chapters; small static text that fades in; desaturated, grain; very few SFX | documentary_dark | slamming text, counters, people icons, colourful charts, pounding music |
| History, biography | 60–80 % archival photos/film, paintings, old maps, artefacts, old newspapers | animated maps (territory, routes), timelines, family trees | medium; slow zoom/pan on stills; warm or B/W | history_warm | wrong-period images, modern illustrations posing as archive, flashy modern fonts |
| Geography, travel, countries, nature | 50–70 % real scenery, aerials, satellite | maps are the backbone: zooms, borders, area comparisons, population/economy | medium, airy; fresh clear colour | vivid | wrong-scale maps or borders, footage from the wrong place |
| Science, technology, how it works | 30–50 % real (experiments, equipment, NASA, microscopes) | 50–70 %: diagrams, simulations, 3D, charts — graphics lead | clear, step by step; motion explains | science_clean | effects that explain nothing, scientifically wrong diagrams |
| Top 10, ranking, comparison | each item has real footage/photos of itself | big rank numbers, info cards, comparison bars, summary table | fast, high energy; strong motion graphics fit | vivid | every item in the same visual, items without real pictures |
| Business, finance, companies | products, HQ, people, launches, articles | revenue/stock charts, money flows, logos, timelines | medium–fast; clean, modern | business_clean | charts without a data source |
| Mystery, legend, the unexplained | places, documents, artefacts | maps, suggestive reconstructions (shadows, blur) | slow, many pauses; dark | mystery_dark | stating the unconfirmed as fact |
| Sports, entertainment, celebrities | real footage/photos of the event and people | scores, stats, records | fast, energetic | vivid | wrong match, season or person |

Mixed titles: pick the main genre and borrow from the second. Unlisted genre: reason the same way — what do
viewers of this genre expect, what makes them trust and stay.

Reference: Challenger = disaster/true story → documentary_dark, target ≈ 60–70 % real; chapters 2–3 (the
engineering) deliberately graphics-heavy because the mechanism had no footage; chapter cards for a 32-min film.
